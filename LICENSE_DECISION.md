# Licence decision required before public release

No open-source or open-data licence has yet been selected for this development
snapshot. Copyright therefore remains with the respective authors and data
providers.

Before making the repository public, the project team should decide separately:

1. **Software licence** — recommended candidates: MIT or BSD-3-Clause.
2. **Documentation licence** — recommended candidate: CC BY 4.0.
3. **Example-data licence** — must follow the licences and redistribution terms
   of the original occurrence and climate-data providers.
4. **Figure licence** — normally aligned with the documentation or publication
   licence.

Do not add a licence merely for convenience. Confirm author agreement, funder
requirements, journal policy and third-party data restrictions first.
