## What is VERA?

VERA is an occurrence-calibrated environmental diagnostic framework. It maps
how strongly local climate departs from a species' occupied reference,
identifies the predictor carrying the leading restriction signal, records
whether that signal is uniquely resolved or shared, and distinguishes high-side
from low-side departures.

VERA combines directionally scaled Variable Restriction Scores with a
covariance-aware Mahalanobis branch. These geometries are complementary and are
not collapsed into one composite score.

> **Interpretive boundary:** VERA does not estimate occurrence probability,
> habitat suitability, physiological tolerance or causal range limitation.

## Tutorial case study

This site demonstrates the workflow using *Sitta krueperi* (Krüper's nuthatch)
under independent 19-predictor and 36-predictor profiles.

The tutorial includes occurrence preparation, ENVIREM generation, asymmetric
calibration, core VERA surfaces, tie-aware attribution, Mahalanobis tiers,
directional add-ons and publication-oriented renderers.
